Gate energy vs thresholds

live energy (dB) trigger threshold hold threshold drag a threshold point to edit

Energy is 10·log₁₀(raw), the same dB scale the thresholds use. A gate trips when energy crosses its trigger line, and stays tripped until it falls below the hold line.

Detection

Ranges are enforced — the module does not range-check and silently truncates out-of-range values to one byte. Response speed and unmanned delay affect only how fast clear is reported, never how fast presence is detected.
